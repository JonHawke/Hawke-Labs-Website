Kestrel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Kestrel is an RSS news reader for Android made by HawkeLabs ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: we don't collect any of your data.

Information we collect

None. Kestrel has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run any servers for the app, and we never receive your feeds, reading history or any other information about you.

Information stored on your device

To work, Kestrel keeps the following on your phone only:

  • The feeds and folders you add, and their website icons
  • Downloaded articles, and which ones you've read or saved
  • Your settings, such as theme, text size, muted words, notification preferences and quiet hours
  • Basic information about the last background check (when it ran and how many articles it found), shown in Settings

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it (see below). It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android's settings.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include Kestrel's data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

Connections the app makes

Kestrel connects directly to websites on your behalf, much like a web browser:

  • Feeds you follow: to check for new articles, including in the background if you turn on background refresh.
  • Article pages: when a feed only includes a summary, Kestrel may load the article's web page to show you the full text. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Images and icons: article images and website icons are loaded from the sites that host them.
  • Adding a feed: when you enter or share a website address, Kestrel visits it to find its feed.

These websites receive the same information as when you visit them in a browser, such as your IP address and the page requested. Their own privacy policies apply. Kestrel does not send them any personal information.

Links you choose to open are shown in your web browser, and the browser's privacy policy applies there.

Files you import or export

If you import or export your feed list (an OPML file), Kestrel only reads or writes the file you pick. Where that file goes afterwards is up to you.

Suggested feeds

The list of suggested feeds is built into the app. Browsing it doesn't send any information anywhere. When you follow a suggested feed, Kestrel connects to that feed as described above.

Permissions

  • Internet: to download feeds, articles and images.
  • Notifications (optional): to tell you about new articles. You can turn notifications off in Settings or in Android's settings at any time.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Google Play may collect a crash report through Android's own systems, depending on your device settings. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy, and we only see anonymous summaries. Kestrel itself contains no crash-reporting or analytics code.

Children

Kestrel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or store any personal data, we hold nothing about you to access, correct or delete. All of your data is on your device and under your control.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
